# Quotation: Update and Finalize Status in Packwares CMS

## # [Packwares CMS](https://cms.packwares.com/manufacturer/quotations)

### 1. On Quotation Page, click on Edit
![Step 1 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-01.png)

### 2. Quotation opened in edit mode.

Check for warnings if settings aren't updated.

![Step 2 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-02.png)

### 3. Parties Overview: Review and Update Details

Update anything that needs correction.

![Step 3 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-03.png)

### 4. Verify Details

Check for correctness and update if required.

![Step 4 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-04.png)

### 5. Update price if required
![Step 5 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-05.png)

### 6. Update Terms and Conditions & Bank Details

Click to update with correct information.

![Step 6 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-06.png)

### 7. Click on Save Changes
![Step 7 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-07.png)

### 8. Scroll up and Preview the updates. 
![Step 8 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-08.png)

### 9. Click on Quotation Preview…
![Step 9 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-09.png)

### 10. Click on Close
![Step 10 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-10.png)

### 11. Click on Back to Quotations
![Step 11 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-11.png)

### 12. Click on Status
![Step 12 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-12.png)

### 13. Select "Ready" form the dropdown

You can choose status as per your choice.

![Step 13 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-13.png)

### 14. Update Comments
![Step 14 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-14.png)

### 15. Click on Update Status
![Step 15 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-15.png)

### 16. Review Quotation List

Check the quotation with updated details and status.

![Step 16 screenshot](./images/5.quotation-update/5.quotation-update-step-16.png)
